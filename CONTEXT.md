# Domain Vocabulary: Lahore Precision Printworks (B2B Printing)

## Core Business
- **B2B Printing Press:** A commercial facility catering to large-volume printing and packaging for FMCG, Pharma, and corporate clients in Lahore.
- **RFQ (Request for Quote):** The core interactive feature allowing clients to estimate costs based on print specifications (quantity, paper weight, finishes).

## Printing Machinery & Fleet (Heidelberg OEM)
- **Speedmaster XL 106-6+L:** The flagship 6-color press with an inline coater. Used for high-speed, premium folding cartons.
- **Speedmaster SM 74-4:** A 4-color press for commercial print (flyers, brochures).
- **Speedmaster CD 102-5:** A 5-color press (CMYK + spot) for heavy board packaging.
- **Cylinder SBG Platen:** A heavy-tonnage machine used for precision hot foil stamping and die-cutting.
- **Suprasetter CTP:** Computer-to-Plate (CTP) thermal plate setter.
- **Polar Mohr 115:** High-speed guillotine paper cutter.

## Substrates & Materials
- **GSM (Grams per Square Meter):** The standard measure of paper weight/thickness. Drives the application recommendation (e.g., 130 GSM for flyers, 350 GSM for folding cartons).
- **Art Card / Bleached Board / SBS (Solid Bleached Sulfate):** Premium, smooth white boards used for high-end packaging.

## Processes & Premium Finishes
- **Spot UV / High-Build UV:** Applying a glossy, raised clear coating over specific design elements for contrast against a matte background.
- **Hot Gold Stamping (Kurz Foil):** Applying metallic foil (often Kurz brand) using heat and a micro-etched brass die.
- **Sculptured Emboss / Deboss:** Creating a raised (emboss) or sunken (deboss) 3D relief on the paper using male/female brass counter dies.
- **Holographic Cold Foil:** An inline process applying prismatic foil that can be overprinted with CMYK ink.
- **Hybrid Drip-Off UV:** A single-pass dual-texture finish creating a matte/sand texture next to high-gloss UV droplets.
- **Die-Cutting:** Using custom steel rules to cut the printed sheet into packaging shapes (e.g., tuck-end carton).

## Application & Formats
- **Commercial Print:** Flyers, leaflets, brochures, menus, presentation folders.
- **Folding Carton:** Cosmetic packaging, pharma boxes, FMCG sleeves.
- **Rigid Box / Setup Box:** Premium gifting and boutique presentation boxes (often using 400+ GSM or rigid board covers).
